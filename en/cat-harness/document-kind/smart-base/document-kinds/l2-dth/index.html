L2 Digital Transformation Handbook (DTH)
description
An L2 Digital Transformation Handbook folio authored on top of an L1 WHO Digital Transformation Handbook and the DPI-H Reference Architecture (smart-ra). It extends the L1 DTH structure with computable L2 linkage and DAK-kind components: every domain term links via SKOS to the shared DPI-H Reference Architecture glossary or an upstream DTH glossary, narrative and structured blocks link to ArchiMate 3.1 elements and views in smart-ra, roles are authored as DAK persona or actor-definition blocks bound to BPMN 2.0 swimlanes, business processes carry BPMN 2.0 companions and structured step tables, decision logic is extracted into DMN 1.3 decision tables citing their source passages, and L1 normative coverage accounts for every normative sentence in the source handbook.

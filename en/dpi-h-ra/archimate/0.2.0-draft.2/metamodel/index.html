  • Timely and Accurate Payments by adopting reliable digital payroll systems
  • Proven Skills by ensuring health worker qualification
  • A Central Health Workforce Registry
  • Continuous Growth by enabling capacity building and fostering career development
  • Ensure effective, equitable and sustainable health financing and public financial management receive reliable digital tools to support intermediate UHC objectives (equitable distribution of resources, efficiency, transparency) and final UHC goals (access in line with need, financial protection, quality of care)
  • Revenue raising for health financing to provide coverage (e.g., taxes, insurance contributions): Secure sustainable, equitable health sector funds.
  • Targeting & identification: Ensure all individuals (across diverse population segments such as vulnerable, informal, and formal) are identified and correctly targeted for coverage and benefits, enabling affordable and equitable access and real-time eligibility decisions.
  • Pooling of funds: Manage pooled funds at scheme level for financial protection, coverage continuity, efficient resource use.
  • Purchasing (including contracting, provider payments, claims management, benefits specification): Ensure affordable, efficient purchasing with timely payments, cost control, transparency.
  • Benefits design: Define coherent entitlements, access conditions, services, populations, price/non-price terms.
  • Safeguarding: Enforce privacy, security, fraud controls across coverage operations.
  • Develop a secure and universally accessible system for issuing verifiable digital health records to individuals for their utilization as they seek care, following appropriate safeguards for privacy, security and consent
  • Support interoperability across providers, facilities, and jurisdictions
  • Establish a secure, universally accessible, verifiable health record for every individual
  • Enable patient control and consent-based sharing of health information
  • Ensure trust, security, and privacy in all transactions
  • Clinical decision support integration
  • Quality monitoring and improvement
  • Patient and family engagement
  • Care team communication
  • Comprehensive Needs Assessment : Systematic evaluation of patient's complete health status, risks, and care requirements.
  • Shared Care Planning: Collaborative development and maintenance of coordinated care plans across all providers.
  • Care Transition Management: Structured handoffs and continuity protocols between care settings and providers.
  • Longitudinal Care Coordination - Ongoing coordination of care activities across time, settings, and disciplines.
  • Budget planning, execution and oversight (monitoring): Ensure health budgets are planned, executed and monitored to support efficient budget execution, transparency and providers paid on time.
  • Health sector monitoring and auditing: Ensure accountable use of health funds through auditability, oversight reporting and appropriate public disclosure
  • Revenue raising (e.g., taxes, insurance contributions): Secure sufficient and sustainable funds for the health sector in an efficient and equitable way
  • Pooling of funds: Allocate pooled health funds equitably across programmes, regions and populations to support redistributive capacity and to achieve financial protection
  • Detect and assess public health threats early
  • Enable timely reporting and coordinated response
  • Strengthen adverse event reporting (AER) and pharmacovigilance
  • Maximize secondary use of health data (for publich health research, and policy)
  • Digitally strengthen public health surveillance and response systems, adverse event reporting, and maximize appropriate secondary use of data for public health purposes.
  • Ensure all health workers receive timely payments by adopting reliable digital payroll systems. This includes maintaining a health workforce registry to ensure qualification, retention, capacity building, and career development.
  • Implement task-sharing and digital decision support systems to improve the continuity and quality of patient care across healthcare facilities over time to achieve integrated, person-centred care.

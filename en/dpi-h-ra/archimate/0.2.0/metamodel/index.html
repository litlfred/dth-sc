  • Adaptive planning, responsive to outbreaks and emergencies

  • Accurate, timely demand forecasts using contextual data
  • Safe health products and services delivered when and where they are needed
  • Complete, timely, and accurate fulfillment of health product orders;
  • Visibility of orders from placement to fulfillment
  • Real-time visibility of stock levels across all warehouses, pharmacies and health facility stores
  • Efficient and accurate processes for receiving, storage, and distribution health products
  • Minimized losses due to expiry, pilferage, or damage
  • Transparent, competitive, and cost-effective procurement processes
  • Health product traceability from supplier to final distribution point
  • Clear performance metrics and accountability for suppliers
  • Monitored reliable suppliers and deliveries
  • Verifiable compliance with contractual terms and quality standards
  • Continuous cold chain temperature monitoring and reporting
  • Cold chain reliability with preventive maintenance and redundancy
  • Minimal vaccine and biologic losses due to cold chain failures.
  • Accurate dispensing to patients based on prescriptions
  • Ttraceability from procurement to patient-level consumption
  • Timely and reliable delivery to all levels of the supply chain
  • Optimized routing and load planning
  • Monitored transportation conditions
  • A Central Health Workforce Registry
  • Timely and Accurate Payments by adopting reliable digital payroll systems
  • Proven Skills by ensuring health worker qualification
  • Continuous Growth by enabling capacity building and fostering career development
  • Ensure all health workers receive timely payments by adopting reliable digital payroll systems. This includes maintaining a health workforce registry to ensure qualification, retention, capacity building, and career development.

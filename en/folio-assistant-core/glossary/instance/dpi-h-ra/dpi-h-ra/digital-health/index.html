Digital Health candidate

The systematic application of information and communications technologies, computer science, and data to support informed decision-making by individuals, the health workforce, and health systems, to strengthen resilience to disease and improve health and wellness.

Also called: digital health

The RA draft's own glossary wording, verbatim (folio/dpi-h-ra/glossary/t-glossary-lead-a31853.md). Substantive and wording alternatives across the DTH corpus (smart-ra#133 S1, smart-ra#140): (1) PHC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240093362-eng/sections/sec-002-glossary.md, p. 8, citing WHO Global Strategy on Digital Health 2020–2025): "Digital health is the systematic application of information and communications technologies, computer science, and data to support informed decision-making by individuals, the health workforce, and health systems, to strengthen resilience to disease and improve health and wellness (11)." (2) SC handbook glossary (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-008.md, p. 8, citing DIIG and Global Strategy): "The systematic application of information and communications technologies, computer science and data to support informed decision-making by individuals, the health workforce and health systems, to strengthen resilience to disease and improve health and wellness (1, 2)." (3) SC handbook Introduction (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-012.md, p. 12, quoting the WHO Global Strategy on Digital Health 2020–2025, same source cited by dth-hrh): "the field of knowledge and practice associated with the development and use of digital technologies to improve health". All are recorded as candidates; none is chosen.

Digital Transformation Handbook for Health Supply Chain Architecture

Digital Health Supply Chain (DHSC) candidate

The knowledge and practice of adopting digital technologies to improve health supply chains and enable the delivery of quality-assured health products to individuals when and where they need them.

Also called: DHSC

Handbook definition, quoted. Open: not adopted, nothing chosen between alternatives. Defined in the Introduction (PDF 12), not in the handbook's glossary.

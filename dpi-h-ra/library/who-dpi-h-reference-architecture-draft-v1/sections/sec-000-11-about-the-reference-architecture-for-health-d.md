Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI-H) Guidance 3 
Most countries are no longer starting from nothing: by 2025, 129 countries1 had developed 4 
national digital health strategies aligned with universal health coverage and the Sustainable 5 
Development Goals. Yet implementation often remains fragmented, with countries lacking a 6 
systematic way to move from high-level health goals to coherent infrastructure decisions. 7 
WHO's Global Strategy on Digital Health 2020-20272 calls on Member States to develop digital 8 
health architectures using enterprise architecture approaches, but guidance at the level of 9 
specificity countries need has not, until now, been widely available. This guidance responds to 10 
that gap. 11

*Table 3. Illustrative cost drivers of DHSC implementation*

| **Cost type** | **Cost category** | **Cost drivers** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ongoing costs | Management | • Complexity of intervention<br>• Full-time equivalents (FTEs) needed<br>• Turnover<br>• Staff capacity |
| Ongoing costs | Governance | • Number of stakeholders needed for coordination<br>• Time needed for approvals<br>• Amount of travel and meetings required for buy-in, coordination and approvals |
| Ongoing costs | Overhead | • Logistical costs such as per diems, meeting rooms and stationery<br>• Administrative costs |
| Upfront implementation costs | Sourcing | • Complexity of required features and functionality<br>• Local capacity and whether international sources need to be explored<br>• Procedural requirements around governmental approvals and competitive evaluations |
| Upfront implementation costs | Software | • Licensing model and associated cost<br>• Scale of implementation (number of end users, number of devices, etc.) |
| Upfront implementation costs | Hardware and infrastructure | • Devices, servers and other infrastructure needed<br>• Sophistication of devices needed (depending on system availability needs, data retention policies, etc.)<br>• Reliability of electricity in country and alternatives needed<br>• Infrastructure to support reliable internet connectivity |
| Upfront implementation costs | Design and development | • Customizations and localizations (e.g., language translations) needed<br>• Local capacity with required skill sets<br>• Technical trainings needed |
| Upfront implementation costs | Integration and interoperability | • Maturity of interoperability standards<br>• Use of standards or lack thereof<br>• Existing data-sharing policies<br>• Licensing fees associated with standard use<br>• Availability of interoperability mediator and related requirements |
| Upfront implementation costs | Testing and training | • Amount of travel and meetings required<br>• Number of end users, testing users and trainers<br>• Scale and frequency of training<br>• Existing capacity gaps |
| Upfront implementation costs | Deployment | • Number of end users<br>• Number of facilities |
| Upfront scaling costs | Expansion of deployment | • Number of end users<br>• Number of facilities |
| Upfront scaling costs | Training | • Amount of travel and meetings required<br>• Number of end users, testing users and trainers<br>• Scale and frequency of training |
| Upfront scaling costs | Hardware and infrastructure | • Devices, servers and other infrastructure needed<br>• Sophistication of devices needed depending on system availability needs, data retention policies, etc.<br>• Reliability of electricity in country and alternatives needed<br>• Infrastructure to support reliable internet connectivity |
| Operations costs | Software | • Annual or per user subscription/licence fees |
| Operations costs | Maintenance | • Amount of bug fixes needed<br>• Anticipated updates released per year<br>• Amount of travel needed for onsite maintenance support<br>• Number of end users and devices managed<br>• Utility costs related to hardware |
| Operations costs | Ongoing training | • On-the-job training vs. formal training mechanism<br>• Turnover |
| Operations costs | Monitoring and evaluation | • Complexity and scope of intervention<br>• FTEs needed to support monitoring and evaluation activities<br>• Amount of data needed to be collected manually vs. data available electronically through automated processes<br>• Size and complexity of data collected; number of systems that data are collected from<br>• Amount of information needed to disseminate |

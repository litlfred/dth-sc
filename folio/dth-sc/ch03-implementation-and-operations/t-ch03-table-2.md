*Table 2. Illustrative implementation considerations for the DHSC*

| **Area** | **Factor** | **What is the current state?** | **What is needed?** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enabling environment | Leadership and governance | • Can any national priorities be cited that align with DHSC goals to secure support from senior leadership?<br>• Does a dedicated department oversee health ICT, digital health and the health supply chain?<br>• Does the country have a national digital governance framework, such as TWGs? | • DHSC that is strongly connected to a national priority to guarantee senior leadership support and momentum<br>• Digital department that has a mandate to advance DHSC implementations<br>• Governance of DHSC implementations by either a dedicated TWG or a subcommittee within the digital health working group, if one exists |
| Enabling environment | Strategy and investment | • Is the DHSC strategy endorsed and agreed upon by all stakeholders?<br>• What investments in DHSC are currently ongoing? What additional investments are planned? | • Consensus among senior leadership and all stakeholders regarding the DHSC vision and strategic roadmap, to ensure alignment of all stakeholder investments with the DHSC architecture<br>• DHSC investment roadmap to help identify funding needs and potential funding sources |
| Enabling environment | Legislation, policy and compliance | • Does any existing legislation support data sharing, data standardization and traceability of pharmaceutical products?<br>• Are policies in place regarding data storage, usage, retention and security?<br>• Do mechanisms exist to ensure compliance with legislation and policies? | • Legislation that provides guidelines to manufacturers and other supply chain partners on product data standardization, labelling, data sharing and pharmaceutical traceability<br>• Guidelines for manufacturers and supply chain partners to achieve compliance in phases<br>• Procedures to monitor and promote compliance with legislation and policies |
| Enabling environment | Workforce | • Are there individuals in supply chain operational and technical roles who have the necessary skill sets?<br>• Are knowledge management mechanisms in place, such as ongoing training and information-sharing programmes?<br>• Is a strategy in place for incremental capacity development and talent retention? | • List of roles across areas such as management, technology and operations with job descriptions and necessary skill sets<br>• Knowledge management procedures that include targeted training programmes, visual job aids, advocacy material and video tutorials<br>• Capacity-building strategy that includes technical cooperation with the private sector and fostering its development to acquire the required skill sets |
| Enabling environment | Standards and interoperability | • Are standardized reusable components in use, such as facility registries, product registries and supplier registries?<br>• Is an interoperability framework in use to guide how systems integrate and exchange data?<br>• Is an interoperability mediator in place to support data orchestration, translation and integrations?<br>• Are standardized protocols in use for data exchange between systems?<br>• Have global and open standards been adopted for data management and exchange? | • List of essential reusable components that are needed as part of the DHSC implementation and that are part of the DHSC architecture<br>• Interoperability framework that includes guidelines for data exchange, standardization and adoption of open standards as well as a plan for an interoperability mediator, if one is not in place<br>• Harmonized master data registries<br>• Adoption of global and open standards (e.g., the Health Level Seven International Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources) to promote interoperability among systems, processes and data |
| ICT environment | Services and applications | • What supply chain applications in use can be enhanced and reused?<br>• Are processes in place to maintain and update software applications? Is a framework in place to engage the private sector in software application maintenance and upgrades? | • DHSC strategy and architecture to indicate existing reusable applications along with new applications, including the necessary changes required for existing applications<br>• Procedures governing application maintenance, including the organization responsible for hardware and software maintenance, criteria for upgrades or replacements and a framework for involving the private sector in maintenance and improvements |
| ICT environment | Infrastructure | • What are the electricity conditions across all supply chain facilities and at data centres where servers will be located?<br>• How is the network connectivity and bandwidth across supply chain facilities such as central warehouses, district pharmacies and service delivery points?<br>• Do potential users of DHSC applications have the necessary devices? If not, what additional devices (e.g., computers, tablets or smartphones) are needed? | • Budgeted plan to establish the required infrastructure for electricity and network connectivity at the designated sites and facilities<br>• Budgeted plan to acquire the required devices for supply chain users<br>• Infrastructure requirements included within government budgets, highlighting potential funding requirements |

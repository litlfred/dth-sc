Source: adapted from the 2012 WHO-ITU National eHealth Strategy Toolkit (19). WHAT IS GOVERNANCE? In the context of the health supply chain, governance is the process of establishing and implementing policies, procedures and mechanisms to ensure that the supply chain processes and the systems to manage those processes are managed effectively, ethically and in alignment with established objectives and stakeholders’ interests. It involves decision-making, defining roles and responsibilities, setting goals and objectives, and monitoring performance to ensure accountability and compliance with regulations and standards.

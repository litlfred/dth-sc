*Annex 3. Template for prioritizing DHSC challenges to address*

| **Bottleneck** | **1. How much impact does this bottleneck have on the process? (1–3)** | **2. What is the likelihood of overcoming this bottleneck? (1–3)** | **3. Is this important to a wide range of stakeholders? (1–3)** | **Score** | **Prioritized ranking** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example: Unavailability of timely consumption data results in inaccurate forecasts and frequent stockouts. | Low (1) | High (3) | Yes (3) | 1+3+3=7 | HIGHEST |

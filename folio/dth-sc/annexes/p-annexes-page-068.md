Annex 3. Template for prioritizing DHSC challenges to address The following table offers some considerations for ranking supply chain challenges and bottlenecks. It can be useful in guiding discussion and reaching consensus among stakeholders. The table describes three hypothetical examples of bottlenecks, which receive scores of Low (1), Medium (2) or High (3) based on responses to three questions. Adding the scores yields the prioritized ranking. Source: adapted from the 2020 WHO Digital Implementation Investment Guide (1).
